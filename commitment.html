Founding Member Commitment

Confidential  ·  April 2026
Agreement

The Network is a closed platform built for credit and funding professionals. At its center is the brain — a living intelligence layer built by founding members, accessed by founding members. This document establishes the standard to which every founding member is held.

Article I
The Rule

Feed the brain to access the brain. Contribution is the only currency in The Network. There are no dues, no fees, no schedules. There is one standard: if you are eating from the brain, you are feeding it.

Article II
Imbalance

There is no schedule for feeding the brain. Members contribute when they have something worth adding — a new bureau tactic, a lender approval, a script that works. That is the natural rhythm of this group. What is not acceptable is consistently taking from the brain without ever putting back into it. A member who only eats loses their seat.

Article III
Confidentiality

Everything inside The Network stays inside The Network. The intelligence, the strategies, the members, the conversations — none of it leaves this room. What goes into the brain was built by this group. It belongs to this group.

By signing below, each founding member commits to this standard — not as a legal obligation, but as a member of something they believe in.
