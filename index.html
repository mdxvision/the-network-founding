Why We Built This

The best intelligence in this industry, organized and working for everyone in this room.

For years the credit and funding space has been fractured. The real playbooks — the bureau intel, the lender sequences that actually work, the strategies that move the needle — lived in individual heads. It didn't scale. It didn't compound. The Network changes that. Everything each founding member brings gets fed into a shared intelligence layer. The agents draw from it in real time. Every operator who comes through benefits from what all of us know.

Shared Intelligence — What We Know Right Now
John Rivero @ TransUnion · April 22
TU Flagging FTC Reports Without Police Reports

TransUnion is now flagging and rejecting FTC identity theft reports submitted without an attached police report. The FTC-only workflow is losing effectiveness with TU. Every submission targeting TransUnion must now include police report documentation. Update your workflows now.

April 22 Founding Call
Bureau Squeeze — Narrowing Dispute Eligibility

All three bureaus are actively reducing which items qualify for dispute and limiting available pathways per item. This is coordinated. The Network's edge is staying ahead of what still works. Your ongoing intelligence contributions are what keep this sharp — bureau tactic updates are a mandatory contribution cadence.

DeVon · April Calls
Equifax Work Number Freeze

A specific technique for freezing the Equifax Work Number — Equifax's employment income verification database — at the bureau level. Prevents lenders from pulling income data during the application process. Highly relevant for clients with income verification situations in lender applications.
