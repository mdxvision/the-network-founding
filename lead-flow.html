The Collective
Lead Flow

One pool. Ten members. Every lead that comes in through a collective campaign gets qualified, routed, and worked using this structure — no exceptions.

Step 2 — Routing
Lane 1 — Has Budget
Direct to Member
Lead answered yes to budget. They go straight into the rotation. Next member in line gets the full lead profile automatically. No delay, no decision, no manual step.
Lane 2 — No Budget
Financing Path
Lead needs help covering the cost. Don't drop them. Based on their credit score and monthly income vs debt, they get routed to the right financing option below.
Lane 2 — Financing Options
Option 1
CoachFinancing
Third-party lender covers the service cost. Member gets paid upfront. Client repays the lender in installments. Best outcome for the member — zero risk, immediate payment.
Member paid upfront
Option 2
Fanbasis
Buy Now Pay Later built into the platform. Client splits the cost into payments without a traditional loan. Score and charge amount determine eligibility.
BNPL
Option 3
Credee
Member creates an in-house payment plan directly with the client. Based on what their income vs debt shows they can afford monthly. No third-party approval needed — member assumes the plan.
In-house plan
⏳
Doesn't Qualify for Any Path
If the income vs debt math doesn't support any payment structure right now, the lead is not dropped — they go into a future follow-up sequence. No hard no. Timing is the only issue.

The Rotation
All 10 members. One pool. Leads rotate in order — no picking, no claiming, no manual assignment. When a lead submits the survey, the next member in rotation gets the profile automatically. Equal distribution, zero delay.
Step 4 — Outcome
Close or Re-Route

Member works the lead. One of two outcomes.

✓
Closed
Service begins. Member reports what worked back to the brain — script, objection handled, financing option used. That intel feeds the whole group.
→
Not Closed
Lead re-routes based on credit score and service charge. Thresholds to be defined. Lead does not die — it moves.
