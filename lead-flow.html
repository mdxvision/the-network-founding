Step 02
Routing — Budget Determines the Lane
Lane 1
Has Budget
Direct to Member
Lead answered yes to budget. They enter the rotation immediately. Next member in line gets the full profile automatically. No delay, no human decision, no manual step.
Lane 2
No Budget
Financing Path
Lead needs help covering the cost. Don't drop them. Credit score + income vs debt determines which financing option fits. Three options below — in priority order.
Lane 2 — Financing Options
Score + Income vs Debt Determines the Route
Option 1 — Best Outcome
CoachFinancing
Third-party lender covers the cost. Member gets paid upfront. Client repays the lender in installments over time. Zero risk to the member — full payment on day one.
Member Paid Upfront
Option 2
Fanbasis
Buy Now Pay Later built into the platform. Client splits the service cost into installments without a traditional loan application. Score and charge amount determine eligibility.
BNPL
Option 3
Credee
Member creates an in-house payment plan directly with the client. Based on what monthly income vs debt shows they can afford. No third-party approval — member owns the plan.
In-House Plan
Doesn't Qualify for Any Path — Nurture, Not Dead
If income vs debt doesn't support any payment structure right now, the lead enters a future follow-up sequence. No hard no. Timing is the only situation. They get re-evaluated when their position changes.
Step 03 — The Rotation
One Pool.
Automatic Assignment.
All 10 members. One queue. When a lead submits the survey, the next member in rotation receives their full profile automatically. No picking. No claiming. No delay. Equal distribution across the entire founding group.

Step 04
Outcome — Close or Re-Route
Closed
Service begins. Member reports back to the brain — what worked, what objection was handled, which financing option was used. That intel feeds the entire group.
Not Closed
Lead re-routes based on credit score and service charge thresholds. Lead does not die — it moves. Thresholds to be defined and seeded into the brain.
